  • Rezoning from C2 to CBD

  • Remarks from Jennifer Bates (City Clerk)

  • Remarks from William Whaley (Planning Commissioner)

  • Long-term planning alignment

  • Rezoning from CBD to C2

  • Long-term planning and growth

  • Parking requirements

    A speaker noted that CBD zoning waives parking requirements unless the property is conditionally used for a hotel, whereas C2 zoning forces parking provision for businesses. Another confirmed that parking is still required even if the property is approved conditionally for a hotel under either zoning code. 39:35

  • Hotel development concerns

  • Parking capacity

    Staff confirmed that the existing parking at the property exceeds the requirement per code, addressing concerns about adding more parking spaces. 42:40
