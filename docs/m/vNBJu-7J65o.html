  • Purpose of the meeting

    The council stated the purpose was to conduct a public hearing on the proposed fiscal year 2027 budget and receive public input. 1:26

  • Public comment rules

    The council instructed attendees to state their name for the record, direct comments to the council, keep them respectful, and limit speech to three minutes. 1:53

  • Internet infrastructure concerns

    A representative of the Blue Ridge Business Association requested the council consider increasing internet in Blue Ridge due to weak connectivity affecting events like 'Blue is a barbecue' and 'Fire and Ice'. They noted that poor internet prevents the use of credit card devices, causing lost revenue for businesses and visitors. 2:12

  • Proposed solutions for internet

    The speaker suggested implementing a city-wide internet use policy, exploring alternative technology such as Starlink, and potentially charging event fees to organizations hosting events to fund the infrastructure. 2:50

  • Funding for internet improvements

    The speaker proposed charging organizations like 'Fire and Ice' an event fee (e.g., $100-$200) to contribute to supporting local internet infrastructure. 3:08
